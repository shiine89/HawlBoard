# Hawlaha kooxda: 3 qof, midkiiba 2 hawlood

Saddexdiinuba branch ka sameeya main. Qof walba ha ku shaqeeyo faylasha u gaar ah. Markaad dhamaysaan, PR u fura main, qof kale ha eego, kadib owner-ku ha merge-gareeyo.

## Qofka 1 (siciid)— Raadinta iyo mudnaanta

Branch tusaale: feature/search-priority

### Hawl 1: Raadi hawlaha
Faylka: src/features/search-filter.js

- Raadi cinwaanka hawsha iyo magaca qofka loo xilsaaray.
- Raadintu ha shaqayso marka qofku wax qorayo.
- Haddii waxba la waayo, board-ku ha muujiyo empty state-kiisa.

### Hawl 2: Shaandhee mudnaanta
Faylka: src/features/priority-filter.js

- Dropdown-ka mudnaanta ha kala shaandheeyo Degdeg, Caadi, iyo Hoose.
- Doorashada Dhammaan mudnaanta ha soo celiso dhammaan hawlaha.
- Raadinta iyo mudnaantu ha isla shaqeeyaan.

## Qofka 2 (shiine) — Abuurista, wax-ka-beddelka, iyo tirtiridda

Branch tusaale: feature/task-editing

### Hawl 1: Hubi xogta form-ka
Faylka: src/features/task-form.js

- Cinwaanka iyo qofka qabanaya ha ahaadaan khasab.
- Haddii xog khaldan tahay, fariin cad ku muuji form-ka.
- Marka hawl la kaydiyo, form-ka nadiifi oo board-ka cusboonaysii.

### Hawl 2: Wax ka beddel hawl jirta
Faylasha: src/features/task-form.js, src/features/task-actions.js

- Badhanka Wax ka beddel ha furo form-ka iyadoo xogta hawsha la doortay ku jirto.
- Kaydintu ha cusboonaysiiso isla hawsha, hana samayn hawl nuqul ah.
- Hubi in badhanka Tirtir ee starter-ka ku jiray uu weli shaqeeyo kadib isbeddellada.

## Qofka 3(Bare) — Dhaqaajinta iyo kaydinta

Branch tusaale: feature/board-storage

### Hawl 1: Jiid oo ku rid
Faylka: src/features/drag-drop.js

- Kaarka hawsha ha laga jiidi karo tiir kale.
- Marka lagu rido tiirka cusub, status-ka hawsha ha is beddelo.
- Tirada tiirarka iyo dashboard-ku ha cusboonaadaan.

### Hawl 2: Xogta kaydi
Faylka: src/features/local-storage.js

- Hawlaha ha ku kaydiyaan browser-ka localStorage.
- Marka bogga dib loo furo, hawlihii hore ha soo noqdaan.
- Haddii browser-ku xog khaldan helo, app-ku ha ku bilaabmo hawlaha demo-ga.

## Sida loo ogaado in hawl dhammaatay

- Isbeddelku wuxuu ka muuqdaa app-ka.
- Shaqada feature-ka waxaa lagu tijaabiyey browser-ka.
- PR-gu wuxuu leeyahay sharaxaad: waxa la beddelay iyo sida loo tijaabiyey.
- PR-ga waxaa eegay ugu yaraan hal qof oo kooxda ka mid ah.
